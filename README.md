# OutPatientDivision_PerformanceQuality_Dashboard
A division wide performance and quality monitoring dashboard that keeps track of outpatient experience in the top private hospital in the Philippines.

## Hospital Operations Context
Private Hospitals in the Philippines operate under three (3) divisions. 
  1. Outpatient
  2. Inpatient
  3. Emergency

The Outpatient Division is made up of 6 clusters: Cluster 1 to 4, Offsite and Operations Support. Each Cluster is made up of several departments - for example, Cluster 1 has Pathology and Laboratory, Nuclear Medicine, Pulmonary Laboratory, Center for Tropical and Travel Medicine. In total, the Outpatient Division consists of 33 Departments, all focused on Outpatient services.

As part of the Operations Support department, under the Outpatient Division, one of the tasks is to monitor the performance quality of each department to ensure that *each patient experiences the highest level of quality in all aspects of the patient journey* from registration, to procedure/extraction, to billing. Being the top private Hospital in the entire country, the hospital is certified by the Joint International Commission (JCI) and follows the international quality standards set by JCI. 

## Problem Statement
Due to the systems  age, structure of the hospital system, and size of the dataset, The hospital currently only monitors the quality performance of each department, individually. *There is no division-wide monitoring dashboard* that senior management can use to check the overall health of the division. This leads to several consequences, namely:

  - A reactionary management, where department leaders only react once the quality level has reached a critical point.
  - A fragmented reporting system that see the operations at a micro-level, rather than at a macro-level.
  - Problems are seen individualistically by cluster leaders and a lack of pattern recognition across clusters and departments.

## Objectives

  1. To create an interactive, division-wide quality monitoring dashboard for the Outpatient   Division.
  2. To create a consolidated database that holds all data points of quality performance for all departments until the available data records
  3. Preserve data integrity through out the entire pipeline and present the performance of each department and the entire division as honestly as possible.
